ERP & SaaS Systems FAQ

What is Ebmspro and where can we try its demo?+

Ebmspro (Easy Business Management System) at ebms.online is our complete ERP & offline-ready POS platform tailored for the Egyptian market — covering branches, inventory variants, accounting, and 14% VAT with an interactive demo on ebms.online.

How do Ebmspro, Easyfy, and Engaz PRO differ?+

Ebmspro (ebms.online) is our full ERP + offline POS for retail, pharmacy & distribution businesses; Easyfy (easyfy.cloud) targets DTC e-commerce brands with Shopify/WooCommerce sync; Engaz PRO (engazpro.com) is our dedicated Salon & Beauty Center booking/operations platform.

Is Samaka Salon a product name or a client?+

Engaz PRO (engazpro.com) is the official Salon & Spa software product; Samaka Salon (samaka-salon.com) is a real commercial client running live daily operations on our Engaz PRO platform.

Do you support custom module extensions and training?+

Yes, we tailor workflows and train your team as part of deployment.
