FAQ

What founders ask before kicking off

Do I own 100% of my store, domain, and data?+

Yes, completely. Domains, admin accounts, customer lists, and cloud licenses are registered under your business with zero lock-in.

How do Easyfy, Ebmspro ERP, and Engaz PRO differ?+

Easyfy (easyfy.cloud) targets e-commerce brands with Shopify/Woo/EasyOrders sync & POS. Ebmspro (ebms.online — Easy Business Management System) is our full ERP & offline-capable POS with branch stock and 14% VAT accounting. Engaz PRO (engazpro.com) is our dedicated Salon & Beauty Center booking & operations suite.

Should we choose Shopify or WordPress / WooCommerce?+

Shopify excels for DTC brands scaling ads with zero hosting maintenance; WordPress/WooCommerce shines for open ownership, corporate portals, and editorial SEO. Both integrate cleanly with Easyfy.

How long does a typical store build take?+

Most Shopify and WordPress launches ship within 5 to 10 business days once brand assets and catalog inputs are ready.

Can we preview the Salon Engaz PRO and Restaurant systems live?+

Yes — full details and demo access for Engaz PRO Salon software are on engazpro.com, live daily at our client Samaka Salon (samaka-salon.com), and our Masa QR dining demo is at masa-qr.site.

Do you provide ongoing support after launch?+

Yes. Every deployment includes post-launch technical support and optional expansion as you open branches or add modules.
